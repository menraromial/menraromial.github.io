## Research

### Thèse (2024–2027)

Contribution à la gestion de la puissance dans les infrastructures cloud : de la limitation matérielle à l'orchestration adaptative. PhD research at Inria / IMT Atlantique.
`Research · Kubernetes · RAPL · Grid'5000 · Power Management · Cloud` - [publications](/publications/)

### Powercap

A power-capping tool leveraging Intel RAPL, designed to plug into Kubernetes as a DaemonSet. Enforces per-node power budgets at the package and DRAM domain level, with audit logs and dry-run mode for cautious operators.
`Go · Kubernetes · RAPL · Linux`

### Bridle

A Kubernetes operator for automatic server power profiling. Bridle observes workload behaviour and builds per-node energy models that feed into scheduling and capping decisions - no manual characterisation required.
`Go · Kubernetes · Operator · Profiling`

### Volta

A custom Kubernetes scheduler plugin that treats energy as a first-class constraint alongside CPU and memory. Volta routes pods to nodes where the marginal energy cost is lowest without violating SLAs.
`Go · Kubernetes · Scheduler · Energy` - [paper](/publications/)
