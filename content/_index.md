# Hi, I'm Menra Romial

I'm a PhD student (also published as *Romial Menra*) at [Inria Rennes](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-universite-rennes) and [IMT Atlantique](https://www.imt-atlantique.fr) (Nantes), working on **power management in cloud infrastructure**, energy-aware computing systems, and sustainable distributed systems. My research focuses on Kubernetes orchestration, efficient server energy management, and optimizing energy consumption based on actual workload demands.

Data centers consume enormous amounts of energy globally, yet significant waste persists due to inefficient resource utilization and suboptimal orchestration strategies. I am driven by the challenge of designing systems that are both performant and energy-efficient - bridging the gap between server capabilities and software-level decision-making.
